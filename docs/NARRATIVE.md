# Site Narrative

The story the site tells, the order it tells it in, and the real copy to build from. Every
line here ran through `antislop` then `writing-as-josh` (monologue register). Voice source of
truth: [`FOUNDER-PROFILE.md`](FOUNDER-PROFILE.md). Strategy: [`../PRODUCT.md`](../PRODUCT.md).

---

## The spine (one throughline)

**Josh is a game maker and a team builder, and they're the same person.** The site proves it
by showing that his games, his leadership, and his ambition all run on one worldview: *carry
responsibility, grow people, tell the emotional truth, waste nobody's time.* A visitor should
leave thinking two things at once: *I'd want this person running my studio,* and *I believe
where he's going.*

The coherence is the argument. A director with a unified worldview is more convincing than a
portfolio. So every section is the same person from a different angle, never a new pitch.

---

## Home — the 30-second arc

Six beats, in this order. The order is the story: **who I am → where I'm going → that I make →
that I lead → that I'm alive and curious → find me.** Each beat is one dominant idea.

### Beat 1 — Hero (identity + direction)

> # I make games that move people. I build teams that own their work.
> I'm a game director building toward a studio of my own. Everything here is how I'm getting
> there.

*Job:* in one fold, the dual identity (maker + builder) and the direction. Two clean
declaratives carry the two halves of him. "Everything here is how I'm getting there" frames the
whole site as evidence, which sets up belief in the vision.

### Beat 2 — The studio I'm building (the ambition, stated plainly)

> **Kicker: Where this goes**
> The studio I'm building is an elite, autonomous cell. A small group that operates at a high
> level entirely on its own, which is what makes it valuable inside a bigger organization or
> standing alone. I want to lead 15 to 20 people who carry real ownership and grow into it.
> That's the work I'm building toward, and everything below is how I'm earning it.

*Job:* name the destination so the proof beats below have a target. Forward conviction, no
tragedy, no contrast. The autonomous-cell framing signals he integrates, not separates.

### Beat 3 — Selected work (proof he makes)

> **Kicker: The work**
> Everything I believe about games, I've built into the ones I make. Start with Zipix.

*Job:* the maker evidence. Zipix leads; other projects follow. Links into `/projects`.

### Beat 4 — How I lead (proof he builds people — the differentiator)

> **Kicker: How I lead**
> People do their best work when the company's goals and their own line up. My job as a leader
> is to find that overlap and widen it. I hand people real responsibility early, more than they
> think they can hold, then I clear the path so they can carry it. The leader grants the
> autonomy. The work supplies the purpose. The worker loves mastering their craft.

*Job:* this is what separates him from every other "game maker." Most portfolios can't speak to
leadership at all. This beat is the one a hiring founder remembers.

### Beat 5 — From the garden (he's alive and curious)

> **Kicker: From the garden**
> The garden is where I think out loud. Side projects, experiments, and the things that pull at
> me. Smaller and more frequent, and still worth your time.

*Job:* range and restlessness. Proof he's a maker who can't stop making. Links into `/garden`.

### Beat 6 — Find me (the magnet close)

> **Kicker: Find me**
> I'm always glad to talk with people building ambitious things in games. If that's you, reach
> out.

*Job:* the call without the begging. Assertive, open, not a hire-me plea. This is "be known and
found" as a sentence.

## Per-page jobs and copy

### /projects — the maker, evidenced

*Job:* depth behind Beat 3. One page per project; Zipix front and center. Each project is
framed as *what belief it puts into practice*, not a feature list.

> **Intro:** The work is the proof. Each of these is a belief I hold, built into something real.

### /resume — the interesting version

*Job:* the credible, skimmable record, told his way, with a clean PDF for recruiters who need
the fast version.

> **Intro:** My resume, the way I'd actually tell it. The short version is on paper if you need
> it.

### /garden — range and curiosity

*Job:* the living feed. Frequent, low-friction, still dense. Even here, no filler.

> **Intro:** Where I think out loud. Half-formed is welcome. Boring is not.

### /presentations — talks and decks

*Job:* the HTML decks, served as themselves, indexed simply.

> **Intro:** Talks and decks I've made. Each one stands on its own.

### /about — the team builder, in full (phase 2)

*Job:* the full leadership thread for the visitor who wants more than Beat 4. Built from
FOUNDER-PROFILE §7: alignment, Autonomy/Mastery/Purpose, stretch-then-clear-path. Starts as the
home beat and graduates to its own page when there's a reason.

## What the narrative deliberately leaves out

- The personal origin of the mission (private fuel, per FOUNDER-PROFILE §6.1).
- Any define-by-contrast framing (monologue register — nothing to correct here).
- Any reference to how the visitor might judge him.
- Hype, hedging, and filler.
