Why games

I've loved games my whole life, playing them and making them. They're the one medium that makes you feel something by doing it yourself. When I make one, I'm chasing that: a small, complete experience you can finish in a sitting and still carry something out of.

Zipix

Where this goes

The studio I'm aiming to build will be an elite, autonomous cell.

A small group that operates at a high level on its own, which makes it valuable inside a bigger organization or standing alone. Leading a great team is how great games get made, and that's why I want to build one.

People do their best work when the company's goals and their own line up. My job as a leader is to find those two directions and align them as tightly as possible. I hand people real responsibility early, more than they think they can hold, then I clear the path so they can carry it. The leader grants the autonomy. The work supplies the purpose. The worker loves mastering their craft.
