# Founder Profile — Josh

**Purpose of this document:** the single source of truth for *who the site is for and who it
represents.* Every decision — copy, structure, aesthetic, motion, what's included and what's
left out — gets checked against this. Built 2026-06-06 from a six-round interview. When a
design choice and this profile disagree, this profile wins (until Josh updates it).

---

## 0. Essence (one line)

A director-track game-studio leader with a unified worldview — *carry responsibility, grow
people, tell the emotional truth, waste nobody's time* — building toward leading studios
that make games which move people to purpose.

---

## 1. The spine — four beliefs that are all the same belief

Josh's taste, leadership, product, and purpose are one worldview in four outfits. This
coherence is the site's most convincing asset. Surface it relentlessly; let a visitor feel
that nothing here is arbitrary.

1. **Carry responsibility.** "Purpose is found by taking on as much responsibility as you
   can without breaking." (His chosen game heroes — 11 bit's *Frostpunk*/*The Alters* — are
   literally about this.)
2. **Grow people.** Leadership and games alike exist to make people more capable, connected,
   and self-possessed. (Why he admires *League of Legends* as a crucible for emotional
   maturity; why he leads by stretching people then clearing their path.)
3. **Tell the emotional truth.** Games as a medium for real feeling — meaning, reflection,
   connection, being seen — not power fantasy. (Why *Clair Obscur: Expedition 33* lands.)
4. **Waste nobody's time.** Density over padding; every decision earns its place; respect
   the player's hours. (Exactly what Zipix is: one minute, no filler.)

**Making is the constant beneath all of it.** Josh loves the craft of making and playing games
for its own sake, not as a means to an end. He would still be making games as a solo dev in
retirement, long after he's done working for anyone. Leadership and the studio are how he
*scales* that love; they never replace it. When the narrative weighs making against leading,
making wins.

## 2. Positioning — two layers, handled differently

**Advertised now (the headline, public, magnetic):** a **studio-caliber leader and maker on a
director track** — someone capable of building and leading a 15–20-person studio that ships
meaningful, time-respecting games. He wants to do this for ambitious companies now, to build
the connections, skills, and capital for what's next. The site should make the right people
think *"I want this person running a team here"* without ever reading as a hard "hire me."

**The trajectory (public, wants belief in it):** heading toward leading — and eventually
founding — an **elite, autonomous studio: a cell that operates at a high level entirely on its
own, and is therefore valuable inside a larger organization or standalone.** Self-sufficient,
not separatist — it plays well with others *because* it doesn't need carrying. Stated as
**forward conviction and ambition.** Visitors should *believe where this is going.*

**The source (PRIVATE — never on the site):** the emotional root of the mission is personal,
including profound loss. **This is fuel, not content.** See §6 hard rules.

## 4. Voice & tone (how every word is written)

- **Bold and declarative. First person. Zero hedging.** No "I think," "I try to," "hopefully,"
  or defensive qualifiers. State facts about identity and intent.
- **Assertive through pure statement.** Confidence shows by what is *absent*: the copy never
  references how a reader might receive it. No "it's fine if you don't like it," no "I have
  nothing to prove," no like-it-or-not. Announcing indifference to opinion is *itself*
  defensive — it's still talking about the audience's judgment. Just state who Josh is and what
  he'll do. The right people resonate; that is left entirely unspoken.
- **Never aggressive, never defensive.** Calm certainty from decades of self-knowledge. Force
  comes from clarity, not volume.
- **Earnest, not ironic; composed, not jokey.** Warmth and conviction, not wit. (See texture.)
- **Antislop, always:** no em-dashes, American spelling. Sincere and plain over clever.

## 5. Self-presentation

**Front and center.** Josh is the protagonist — face, first person, worldview as the main
attraction. The work (Zipix, projects, presentations) is the *evidence* for the person, not a
substitute for him.

**Human texture to convey (so he reads as a person, not a mission statement):**
- **Relentless drive** — intensity, work ethic, will-not-be-stopped.
- **Steady & grounded** — calm, considered, hard to rattle; self-knowledge as composure.
- **Warm & generous** — gives ownership, credit, and care; people visibly matter.
- *Not* comedic. Lightness, if any, comes through craft and care, not jokes.

---

## 6. Hard rules (do-not-cross)

1. **No personal tragedy on the site, ever.** The brother, grief, and loss are private fuel.
   Using tragedy as marketing is beneath Josh's bar; he wins on merit, never pity. The mission
   may be public; its painful origin may not.
2. **Never reference the audience's reception.** No "it's fine if you don't like it," no "I
   have nothing to prove," no defending or pre-empting judgment — even stating indifference is
   defensive, because it still addresses the reader's opinion. Pure statement of identity and
   intent: no argument, no over-explaining, no credential-stacking for reassurance.
3. **The alive-interaction motif is craft, not comedy.** Subtle, considered, "more is alive
   than you expected" — a signal that he sweats the details. Not winking gags. (Reconsider the
   mockup's "type josh" easter egg through this lens.)
4. **No filler.** The site itself must respect the visitor's time, the way his games do. Dense
   with signal, cut the padding. The medium practices the message.

## 7. Leadership philosophy (for the team-builder thread / `/about`)

- **Best work happens when company goals and personal goals align.** A manager's job is to find
  and widen that overlap.
- **Drives internal motivation via Autonomy, Mastery, Purpose** (Pink's *Drive*), with a
  personal routing: *autonomy* is granted by the manager, *purpose* supplied by the
  org/product, *mastery* earned by the person chasing what pulls them.
- **Leads by stretching + clearing.** Hands real ownership early, stretches people past what
  they thought they could carry — with a net so they don't break — then removes obstacles and
  lets them shine (servant-leadership). Holds the "why" most strongly when he believes the
  mission or owns it.
- This is the same belief as the games: **purpose through responsibility, applied to people.**
