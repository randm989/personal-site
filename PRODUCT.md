register: brand

## Product purpose

A personal site that makes Josh **known and found** as a director-track game-studio leader.
Near-term job: maximize surface area for the right strangers, companies, and collaborators to
discover him. It advertises the leader companies want *now* (build and lead a 15-20 person
studio) and makes the right visitor *believe the long-term vision*. A magnet, not a job
application.

## Users

The visitor decides in about 30 seconds. Four kinds:

1. **Hiring leaders / founders at game companies** — looking for someone to direct or lead a
   team. The primary target. They should leave thinking "I'd want this person running my studio."
2. **Potential collaborators / cofounders** — builders who want to make ambitious games with him.
3. **Press / curious strangers** — surface area for luck and reputation.
4. **Recruiters** — who need the clean, skimmable version fast (and the resume PDF).

## Brand voice and tone

Bold, first-person, declarative, zero hedging. Assertive but never aggressive, never defensive.
Earnest and grounded, warm about people. Confidence from clarity, not volume.

**The voice is a built skill:** apply `writing-as-josh` (monologue register) for every word a
visitor reads, as the pass after `antislop`. Three allergies, never admitted: **filler, pity,
try-hard.** Never reference how the reader might receive it.

## Anti-references (what this must never become)

- **Pity / tragedy as marketing.** The personal origin of the mission is private fuel, never
  on the site. He wins on merit.
- **Try-hard / something-to-prove.** No reaching, no credential-stacking, no defending.
- **Generic-founder template.** Centered hero, icon-title-subtitle cards, "passionate about."
- **AI slop / editorial-magazine reflex.** "Editorial" here means calm and confident, NOT the
  saturated display-serif-italic + mono-label + ruled-column lane.
- **Filler.** The site respects the visitor's time the way his games do.

## Strategic principles

1. **The four beliefs are the spine** (carry responsibility, grow people, tell the emotional
   truth, waste nobody's time). His taste, leadership, product, and purpose are one worldview.
   Surface the coherence relentlessly.
2. **The medium practices the message.** The site respects the visitor's time (no filler), and
   it feels quietly alive as a craft signal (the motif is care, never comedy).
3. **Front and center.** Josh is the protagonist; the work is his evidence.
4. **Two-layer positioning:** director-grade leader *now*, visionary worth betting on *later*.
   Dual takeaway: "I'd want them running my studio" AND "I believe where this is going."
